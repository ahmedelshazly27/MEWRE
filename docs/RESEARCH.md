# Kuwait Renewable Energy Sector: Research Brief

**Prepared:** 2026-10-19
**Purpose:** The factual basis for a learning platform that prepares a renewable-energy Project Manager at Kuwait's Ministry of Electricity, Water and Renewable Energy (MEWRE).

**Method and caveats**
- All facts below came from web searches run on 2026-10-19. Direct page fetching was unavailable in the research environment because of DNS failures. Most facts therefore rest on search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not on full-text reading.
- Each bullet names its source and the source's date as reported. Where the date could not be confirmed, the bullet says "date n/c" (not confirmed).
- **[UNVERIFIED]** marks a claim that has only one weak source, conflicts with other sources, or could not be confirmed.
- Before using any number in assessed course content, re-check it against the primary source: KAPP (kapp.gov.kw), MEWRE (mew.gov.kw), Kuwait Al-Youm (the official gazette), or KUNA.
- No figures were invented. Where a figure was not found, the brief says so.

## 1. MEWRE: history, structure, leadership, role

### History and name
- The ministry was established on **17 January 1962** as the Ministry of Electricity and Water. "Renewable Energy" was added to its name later. [Ministry of Electricity, Water and Renewable Energy (Kuwait), Wikipedia, accessed 2026-10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Electricity,_Water_and_Renewable_Energy_(Kuwait))
- **[UNVERIFIED]** The date and number of the decree that added "Renewable Energy" to the name were not found. The new name is in use in official 2024–2025 decrees, such as the August 2024 reshuffle below. Check Kuwait Al-Youm or the Amiri Diwan archive.
- In January 2023 the government announced a plan to convert MEWRE into a corporation, with subsidiaries for electricity, water and renewables. [Kuwait plans to transform its Electricity, Water and Renewable Energy Ministry into a corporation, SolarQuarter, 2023-01-18](https://solarquarter.com/2023/01/18/kuwait-plans-to-transform-its-electricity-water-and-renewable-energy-ministry-into-a-corporation/). **[UNVERIFIED]** No evidence was found that this conversion has been carried out.

### Structure
- One report says the ministry has about 11 subdivisions, covering transmission, power stations, project tendering, water distribution and electrical distribution. [Ministry of Electricity, Water and Renewable Energy (Kuwait), Wikipedia, accessed 2026-10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Electricity,_Water_and_Renewable_Energy_(Kuwait)). The official org chart is at [Organizational Structure, MEW Kuwait (date n/c)](https://www.mew.gov.kw/en/about/organizational-structure/). **[UNVERIFIED]** The exact names of the renewable-energy sector units were not confirmed.
- Undersecretary **Adel Al-Zamel** signed the March 2025 framework agreement with China's National Energy Administration for MEWRE. [Kuwait seeks Chinese help with solar expansion, MEES, 2025-03-21](https://mees.com/2025/3/21/power-water/kuwait-seeks-chinese-help-with-solar-expansion/5534ff50-0650-11f0-b115-337ee0d38b8c)

### Recent ministers
- **March 2024:** Minister **Salem Al-Hajraf** unveiled the 2030–2050 renewable energy strategy on 7 March 2024. [Kuwait playing catch-up with latest renewables strategy, AGBI, 2024-03](https://www.agbi.com/analysis/renewable-energy/2024/03/kuwait-playing-catch-up-with-latest-renewables-strategy/)
- **25 August 2024:** A cabinet reshuffle decree named **Mahmoud Abdulaziz Bushehri** Minister of Electricity, Water and Renewable Energy. [Kuwaiti Amiri decree reshuffles cabinet, QNA/KUNA, 2024-08-25](https://www.qna.org.qa/en/News-Area/News/2024-08/25/0062-kuwaiti-amiri-decree-reshuffles-cabinet)
- **10 March 2025:** The previous minister's resignation was accepted, and the Minister of Public Works was made acting minister. [46th Cabinet of Kuwait, Wikipedia, accessed 2026-10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/46th_Cabinet_of_Kuwait); [Electricity minister's resignation accepted, Kuwait Times, 2025-03](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/25333/kuwait/other-news/electricity-ministers-resignation-accepted/amp)
- **24 March 2025:** An Amiri decree appointed **Dr. Sabeeh (Subaih) Al-Mukhaizeem** as Minister of Electricity, Water and Renewable Energy. [Amiri decree assigns Sabeeh Al-Mukhaizeem as Electricity, Water, Renewable Energy min., Amiri Diwan, 2025-03-24](https://www.da.gov.kw/en/news/1423-24_03_2025/)
- He was also acting Finance Minister from 4 August 2025. A different person has been Finance Minister since 1 February 2026. [46th Cabinet of Kuwait, Wikipedia, accessed 2026-10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/46th_Cabinet_of_Kuwait)
- He was still acting as minister on 20 May 2026. [Embassy of Azerbaijan in Kuwait news, 2026-05-20](https://kuwait.mfa.gov.az/en/news/sefir-emil-kerimov-kuveyt-dovletinin-elektrik-enerjisi-su-ve-berpa-olunan-enerji-naziri-ile-gorusub). He is also cited for the summer-2026 peak figures (section 2). **[UNVERIFIED]** It is not confirmed that he is still in office as of October 2026.

### Role as offtaker and procurer
- MEWRE is the offtaker for IPP and IWPP projects:
  - Solar IPPs use a **30-year PPA**. [Kuwait reveals prequalified bidders for 1.1 GW solar project, pv magazine, 2025-06-16](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2025/06/16/kuwait-reveals-prequalified-bidders-for-1-1-gw-solar-project/)
  - IWPPs use a **25-year ECWPA** (Energy Conversion and Water Purchase Agreement). [Kuwait finalizes contract for new Az-Zour power plant, MEES, 2026-02-06](https://www.mees.com/2026/2/6/news-in-brief/kuwait-finalizes-contract-for-new-az-zour-power-plant/55f73240-036a-11f1-9cf7-1f1411c486a3)
- MEWRE also procures grid works directly through conventional public tenders. Example: the Shagaya–Wafra 400 kV line awarded in March 2026 (section 5). [Kuwait awards $158.7 million 400kV OHTL contract, Megaproject (date n/c, reports March 2026 award)](https://megaproject.com/news/oilandgas/kuwait-awards-158-7-million-400kv-ohtl-contract)

### Installed capacity and generation mix
- Installed capacity is about **21 GW**, but only about **17 GW** is reliably available at peak because of maintenance and plant age. Gas-fired capacity is about 14 GW. These are Rystad figures. [Kuwait's ambitious renewable energy goals face reality check, Invezz, 2025-09-16](https://invezz.com/news/2025/09/16/kuwaits-ambitious-renewable-energy-goals-face-reality-check/)
- Ember data via Inzonex puts the generation mix at about **62% gas, 36% oil, about 2% renewables**. [Kuwait power atlas, Inzonex (date n/c)](https://inzonex.co.uk/poweratlas/kuwait/). This is a third-party source, so confidence is medium.
- A Baker Institute paper says more than 99% of power comes from fossil fuels, including crude oil and refined products. [Distortionary effects of Kuwait's cheap electricity, Baker Institute (date n/c, cites 2022 data)](https://www.bakerinstitute.org/research/distortionary-effects-kuwaits-cheap-electricity-and-case-just-reform)
- Largest plants in a WRI-based listing: Az-Zour South about 5,805 MW, Sabiya about 5,366 MW, Doha West about 2,543 MW. Inzonex (above), medium confidence.
- The minister said Kuwait will add **14.05 GW** of generation by 2031, including Az-Zour North 2&3. [Zawya, 2025](https://www.zawya.com/en/business/energy/kuwait-to-target-1405-gw-power-generation-capacity-by-2031-bwawltqz); [Kuwait plans 14GW to meet soaring power demand, AGBI, 2025-09](https://www.agbi.com/energy/2025/09/kuwait-plans-14gw-to-meet-soaring-power-demand/)
- **Renewable capacity:**
  - About 102 MW of solar at end-2024. [Theenergyyear interview, Ahmad Al-Dousari (MEWRE), 2025](https://theenergyyear.com/articles/towards-full-realisation-of-kuwaits-renewables-potential/)
  - IRENA reports total renewables of 114 MW at end-2025, unchanged from 2024. This was relayed via a search summary. **[UNVERIFIED]** It has not been checked against IRENA directly.
  - Rystad puts solar at about 50 MW. [Kuwait forecast to reach 2.9 GW of solar by 2030, pv magazine, 2025-09-24](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2025/09/24/kuwait-forecast-to-reach-2-9-gw-of-solar-by-2030-10-1-gw-by-2035/)
  - **The sources conflict.** The figures differ in scope: solar only versus all renewables, and with or without CSP.

### Power cuts and load-shedding
- **June 2024:** On 19 June 2024 a 300 MW steam turbine at Az-Zour South tripped. MEW announced scheduled cuts of up to 2 hours a day across multiple areas and expected them to last at least two weeks. [MEES, 2024-06-21](https://www.mees.com/2024/6/21/news-in-brief/kuwait-power-crisis/5ccd1920-2fd7-11ef-92c2-0be19dd98bc9); [Kuwait forced to cut power to some areas as heat raises demand, Bloomberg/BNN, 2024-06-19](https://bnnbloomberg.ca/kuwait-forced-to-cut-power-to-some-areas-as-heat-raises-demand-1.2087181). Kuwait Times reported more than 40 residential areas hit. [Rolling power cuts hit Kuwait, Kuwait Times, 2024-06](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/15693/kuwait/other-news/rolling-power-cuts-hit-kuwait)
- **August 2024:** Rolling cuts followed a gas supply disruption. [Heat blackouts blamed on Kuwait's fuel supply issues, AGBI, 2024-08](https://www.agbi.com/energy/2024/08/heat-blackouts-blamed-on-kuwaits-fuel-supply-issues/)
- **April 2025:** Cuts hit more than 60 residential, agricultural and industrial areas during an early heat spike while units were in maintenance. [Kuwait enforces power cuts as demand outstrips capacity, AGBI, 2025-04](https://www.agbi.com/energy/2025/04/kuwait-enforces-power-cuts-as-demand-outstrips-capacity/)
- **May 2025:** Widespread cuts at the 16.58 GW peak. The ministry ran programmed 1–2 hour cuts and asked consumers to reduce use between 11:00 and 17:00. [MEES, 2025-05-23](https://www.mees.com/2025/5/23/power-water/kuwaits-power-generation-fragility-exposed-as-demand-soars/defefea0-37ca-11f0-bbd3-c75b80d6be75)
- Shortages exceeded 1.5 GW during the May 2025 peak. [Invezz/Rystad, 2025-09-16](https://invezz.com/news/2025/09/16/kuwaits-ambitious-renewable-energy-goals-face-reality-check/)
- **[UNVERIFIED]** No reports of summer-2026 load-shedding were found.

### Imports via GCCIA

[Kuwait signs 500MW GCCIA power import contract, MEES, 2024-05-24](https://www.mees.com/2024/5/24/news-in-brief/kuwait-signs-500mw-gccia-power-import-contract/c50faa20-19d9-11ef-b8e3-c9e32c4d29c6)

[Kuwait secures approval for GCCIA imports, Powerline, 2025-07-28](https://powerline.net.in/2025/07/28/kuwait-secures-approval-for-gccia-imports-to-stabilise-summer-power-supply/)
- **[UNVERIFIED]** A 2024 report expected the Al-Wafra 400 kV substation and its Saudi link to be operational by December 2024. No commissioning confirmation was found.

### Tariffs and subsidies
- **Kuwaiti citizens (private housing): 2 fils/kWh**, the baseline since 1966. Baker Institute puts this at about 0.7 US cents/kWh. [Baker Institute (date n/c)](https://www.bakerinstitute.org/research/distortionary-effects-kuwaits-cheap-electricity-and-case-just-reform)
- The **2016 tariff law** (passed April 2016) exempts citizens. For apartment buildings, mainly occupied by expatriates:
  - 5 fils/kWh for the first 1,000 kWh a month
  - 10 fils/kWh for 1,000–2,000 kWh
  - 15 fils/kWh above 2,000 kWh

  Source: [Lawmakers exempt citizens from proposed raises, Kuwait Times, 2016](https://kuwaittimes.com/lawmakers-exempt-citizens-proposed-raises)
- The ministry later scaled the rates back. **Commercial: 5 fils/kWh** (instead of 25). **Industrial and agricultural: 3 fils/kWh**. [Planned increases in electricity charges sharply reduced, Kuwait Times, ~2016–17 (date n/c)](https://kuwaittimes.com/planned-increases-electricity-charges-sharply-reduced-mp). Apartment charges took effect from 22 August 2017. [New power, water tariffs applied Aug 22, Kuwait Times, 2017](https://kuwaittimes.com/new-power-water-tariffs-applied-aug-22)
- **[UNVERIFIED]** The law number is often cited as Law No. 20 of 2016, but this was not confirmed. Whether the apartment tiers were reduced to a flat 5 fils is also not confirmed: one Kuwait Times report says apartments were capped at 5 fils. No 2025–2026 tariff change was found.
- **Production cost:**
  - About 38 fils/kWh in an older estimate (~2014–2015). [Rising cost, Oxford Business Group, 2015](https://oxfordbusinessgroup.com/rising-cost-growing-demand-has-prompted-a-drive-to-boost-generating-capacity-and-explore-alternatives/)
  - 14–26 fils/kWh in an ERF paper. [ERF working paper 0405, 2017](https://erf.org.eg/app/uploads/2017/04/0405_final.pdf)
  - Electricity subsidies cost about KD 3.1bn in 2013 (OBG, above). Fuel for subsidized power cost more than USD 10bn in 2022 (Baker Institute).
  - **The sources conflict, and no current official cost figure was found.**
- **KPC tariff:** KPC pays about **25 fils/kWh** for grid power. It uses about 1.7 GW of roughly 17 GW of supply. Analysts estimate solar at 7–8 fils/kWh. [Kuwait Petroleum Corporation to launch 2 GW solar project, SolarQuarter, 2026-01-29](https://solarquarter.com/2026/01/29/kuwait-petroleum-corporation-to-launch-2-gw-solar-project-cutting-costs-and-carbon-emissions/)

### Fuel
- Kuwait burns fuel oil, gasoil and crude for power, mainly in summer. Rising gas output is expected to push liquids burn to its lowest in more than 20 years. Kuwait imported **5.2 Mt of LNG in January–August 2025**, against 4.9 Mt a year earlier (Vortexa data). [Rising gas output helping to curb Kuwaiti oil burn, Argus, 2025-09-19](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2733686-rising-gas-output-helping-to-curb-kuwaiti-oil-burn)
- Qatar LNG contracts: 3 Mt/yr for 15 years from 2022 (signed 2020), plus another 3 Mt/yr for 15 years from 2025 (signed 2024). Argus (above); [Qatar to provide LNG to Kuwait for 15 years, Cyprus Mail, 2024-09-01](https://cyprus-mail.com/2024/09/01/qatar-to-provide-lng-to-kuwait-for-15-years)
- **[UNVERIFIED]** No barrels-per-day figure for crude or fuel-oil burn in power was found.

## 3. Targets

- **15% renewables by 2030:** Set in 2012 and the long-standing target, still widely cited. [Theenergyyear, Al-Dousari (MEWRE), 2025](https://theenergyyear.com/articles/towards-full-realisation-of-kuwaits-renewables-potential/); [pv magazine, 2025-09-24](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2025/09/24/kuwait-forecast-to-reach-2-9-gw-of-solar-by-2030-10-1-gw-by-2035/)
- **Updated path of 30% by 2030, 40% by 2040, 50% by 2050:** Stated by MEWRE official Ahmad Al-Dousari in a July 2025 interview. [Theenergyyear, 2025](https://theenergyyear.com/articles/towards-full-realisation-of-kuwaits-renewables-potential/)
- **50% by 2050:** Also cited by the minister. [China, Kuwait sign agreement to expand solar projects, Arab News, 2025-03](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2594037/)
- **Capacity under the 2030–2050 strategy:** Unveiled 7 March 2024 by Minister Al-Hajraf, with about **22 GW** of renewables cited "by the end of the decade". [AGBI, 2024-03](https://www.agbi.com/analysis/renewable-energy/2024/03/kuwait-playing-catch-up-with-latest-renewables-strategy/). **[UNVERIFIED / likely ambiguous]:** 22 GW by 2030 is implausible against a 17.5 GW peak. The figure may refer to 2050. Treat it with caution.
- **KOC/KBR masterplan:** 17 GW of renewables and 25 GW of green hydrogen by 2050. This is an oil-sector planning study, not an official national target. [Kuwait targets 17 GW of renewables and 25 GW of green hydrogen by 2050, Enerdata (date n/c)](https://www.enerdata.net/publications/daily-energy-news/kuwait-targets-17-gw-renewables-and-25-gw-green-hydrogen-2050.html)
- **Net zero:**
  - Kuwait pledged **carbon neutrality by 2060**, and net zero for the **oil and gas sector by 2050**. The Foreign Minister announced both at **COP27** (Sharm El-Sheikh, November 2022). [Kuwait commits to net zero by 2060, KnowESG, 2022-11-09](https://knowesg.com/cop27/kuwait-commits-to-achieving-net-zero-emissions-by-2060-09112022); [Kuwait aims to reach net zero emissions by 2060, Enerdata, 2022-11](https://www.enerdata.net/publications/daily-energy-news/kuwait-aims-reach-net-zero-emissions-2060.html)
  - KPC targets net-zero Scope 1 and 2 by 2050. [KPC unveils sustainability report, Kuwait Times (date n/c)](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/51255/kuwait/other-news/kpc-unveils-sustainability-report-targets-net-zero-emissions-by-2050/)
  - **Conflict:** Some outlets write "2050 net zero" for the whole country. The 2050 date is the oil and gas sector target.
- **NDC:** The updated NDC (October 2021) commits to avoiding **7.4% of 2035 BAU emissions** through unconditional effort. [Kuwait Updated NDC 2021, via ESMAP RISE](https://rise.esmap.org/data/files/library/kuwait/Renewable%20Energy/Kuwait_Updated%20NDC_2021.pdf). The Environment Public Authority launched a 2050 Low-Carbon Strategy in November 2023 (Enerdata/KnowESG coverage).
- **Outlook:** Rystad forecasts about 3.3 GW of renewables by 2030 (about 7% of generation) and about 11 GW by 2035. It expects the 15% target to be missed in 2030 and perhaps reached around 2035. [pv magazine, 2025-09-24](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2025/09/24/kuwait-forecast-to-reach-2-9-gw-of-solar-by-2030-10-1-gw-by-2035/); [Rystad: 3.3 GW by 2030, SolarQuarter, 2025-09-16](https://solarquarter.com/2025/09/16/rystad-energy-says-kuwaits-renewable-capacity-to-reach-just-3-3-gw-by-2030-falling-short-of-15-electricity-target/amp/)

## 4. Shagaya Renewable Energy Park

### Overview and original masterplan
- The park is in Jahra Governorate, west of Kuwait City. The site is about **84 km²**. [Confusion surrounds Kuwait's mega solar plans, pv magazine, 2021-01-06](https://pv-magazine.com/2021/01/06/confusion-surrounds-kuwaits-mega-solar-plans)
- The original IEA-listed plan (2014) had three phases:
  - Phase 1: 70 MW
  - Phase 2: 1,000 MW by about 2020 (550 MW CSP, 380 MW PV, 70 MW wind)
  - Phase 3: 2,000 MW total by 2030 (1,150 MW CSP, 723 MW PV, 127 MW wind)

  Source: [Development and Deployment of Utility Scale Renewable Power Plant, IEA Policies database](https://www.iea.org/policies/5541)
- A later restructured plan had a Phase 2 CSP plant of 200 MW with about 5 hours of storage, a 1,500 MW solar IPP, and a final 1,700 MW solar IPP. [ICE news item (date n/c)](https://www.ice.it/it/news/notizie-dal-mondo/259540). The 200 MW CSP Phase 2 was **never procured**. **[UNVERIFIED]** No source documenting its formal cancellation was found.

### Phase I (KISR pilot, 70 MW)
- **Configuration:** 50 MW CSP (parabolic trough), 10 MW PV, 10 MW wind. [IEA policies 5541](https://www.iea.org/policies/5541); [Kuwait launches solar power project, Gulf Construction Online (date n/c)](https://gulfconstructiononline.com/Article/382141/Kuwait_launches_solar_power_project)
- **CSP:** Parabolic trough with **10 hours of molten-salt thermal storage** and dry cooling. [CSP makes a grand entry into Kuwait, Reuters Events (date n/c, ~2015)](https://www.reutersevents.com/renewables/node/55702). Worley/Advisian gives the storage as **1,200 MWh-thermal**. [Shagaya Renewable Energy Project case study, Worley (date n/c)](https://insights.worley.com/case-studies/shagaya-renewable-energy-project). Both are consistent.
- **Target output:** Up to about 225,000 MWh a year. Reuters Events (above).
- **Commissioning:**
  - Wind: May 2017.
  - CSP: late 2018, with commercial operation / full operation from **February 2019**. [GDN Online (date n/c)](https://www.gdnonline.com/Details/1379279); [Kuwait eyes up to 400 MW of CSP in expanded solar park, Reuters Events, 2019](https://www.reutersevents.com/renewables/node/61064)
  - **Conflict:** the exact dates differ by source.
- **Contracts:**
  - Wind: Elecnor, about EUR 22.4m. [Elecnor wins €22.4M contract, IPP Journal (date n/c, ~2015)](https://ippjournal.com/news/elecnor-wins-22-4-m-contract-to-build-maintain-kuwait-s-first-wind-farm)
  - CSP: TSK, with KISR approval reported in 2015. [Kuwait da el OK a TSK, Energías Renovables, 2015-09-15](https://www.energias-renovables.com/termosolar/kuwait-da-el-ok-a-tsk-para-20150915)
  - Contracts included 6-year design-build-operate periods (Reuters Events).
  - **[UNVERIFIED]** The CSP EPC partners (TSK and Kharafi?) are not confirmed by the sources found.
- **Wind performance** (five 2 MW turbines):
  - Long-term mean wind speed **7.9 m/s at 80 m**.
  - Capacity factor 45.2% in year 1 (about 40 GWh) and 42.1% in year 2 (about 37 GWh). About 70% in summer months.

  Source: [Performance analysis of a 10 MW wind farm in a hot and dusty desert, peer-reviewed (UCAR impacts listing, ~2021)](https://impacts.ucar.edu/en/publications/performance-analysis-of-a-10-mw-wind-farm-in-a-hot-and-dusty-dese/)

  A KISR conference paper reports **42.59 GWh in year 1, 21% above the 31.16 GWh contractual figure**. [E3S Conf. ICSF 2021, 05016](https://www.e3s-conferences.org/articles/e3sconf/pdf/2021/56/e3sconf_icsf2021_05016.pdf). **Conflict:** the two studies differ slightly on period and method. **Arithmetic note:** 42.59 / 31.16 = 1.37, i.e. 37 % above, not the 21 % stated in the paper; 42.59 GWh on 10 MW also implies a 48.6 % capacity factor vs the 45.2 % reported elsewhere. Treat the exact figures as unverified.
- **PV testbed:** A KISR-linked paper describes an approximately 11.15 MW testbed comparing thin-film and polycrystalline modules. Soiling is "substantial" and requires **monthly water cleaning**. [Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews vol. 133, 2020 (IDEAS listing)](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/rensus/v133y2020ics1364032120304305.html)
- **[UNVERIFIED]** No published operating data or availability for the 50 MW CSP plant after 2019 was found, and no current operating status (2025–2026).

### Phase III (Al Dibdibah Power and Al Shagaya Renewable Energy), procured by KAPP

**Background**
- KNPC originally tendered the Al-Dibdibah (Dabdaba) solar project for oil-sector use under EPC+F. Bids came in April 2019 at about 1.5 GW, with seven bids. ## 6. Institutions and legal framework

- **KAPP (Kuwait Authority for Partnership Projects):**
  - Established under **PPP Law No. 116 of 2014**. Executive Regulations are **Decree No. 78 of 2015**. The law replaced Law 7/2008.
  - KAPP is attached to the Minister of Finance.
  - The **Higher Committee for PPP**, chaired by the Minister of Finance, sets policy, approves projects and procurement stages, and approves the selected investor on KAPP's recommendation.

  Sources: [PPP Law 116 of 2014 (unofficial English translation), KAPP](http://www.kapp.gov.kw/uploads/lagislation/PPP%20Law%20116%20of%202014.pdf); [Executive Regulations Decree 78/2015, KAPP](http://www.kapp.gov.kw/uploads/lagislation/Executive%20Regulations.pdf); [2024 Investment Climate Statement: Kuwait, US State Dept., 2024](https://2021-2025.state.gov/reports/2024-investment-climate-statements/kuwait/)
- **Typical PPP process** (synthesised from sources):
  1. Public entity request
  2. KAPP feasibility study
  3. Higher Committee approval
  4. RFQ and prequalification
  5. RFP with technical, then financial, bid opening
  6. Preferred bidder
  7. Higher Committee approval
  8. **State Audit Bureau** approval
  9. Signing of the PPA/ECWPA
  10. Financial close

  Sources: [Kuwait PPP 10 years on, IJGlobal (date n/c)](https://ijglobal.com/articles/132146/kuwait-ppp-10-years-on); [MEED schools-PPP coverage (date n/c)](https://www.meed.com/exclusive-kuwait-awaits-final-approval-schools-ppp-contract)

## 7. The PPP/IPP model in Kuwait

- **Shareholding under Law 116/2014** (projects above KD 60m), from a secondary legal guide:
  - **50%** offered by IPO to Kuwaiti citizens
  - **6–24%** to public entities
  - **at least 26%** to the private investor

  KAPP warehouses the IPO shares until the project is operating. Unsubscribed shares stay with KAPP. [Legal 500 guide](https://www.legal500.com/developments/thought-leadership/entry-of-major-corporations-into-kuwait/); [Public-Private Partnership Projects in Kuwait, Al Tamimi (date n/c)](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update-articles/public-private-partnership-projects-in-kuwait/); [Mondaq (date n/c)](https://www.mondaq.com/Article/422586)

  **Conflict:** The brief's prompt said "6–26% government / 24–44% private". The legal guide says 6–24% public and at least 26% private. Verify against the law text.
- **In practice (IWPPs):** The developer consortium holds **40%** and the state, through KAPP, **60%**, with 50% earmarked for a public IPO. Examples: Az-Zour North 1, and Az-Zour North 2&3 (40/60).
- **Az-Zour North 1 precedent:**
  - About 1,500–1,540 MW plus desalination. Owner: Shamal Az-Zour Al-Oula KSC.
  - Private 40%: ENGIE 17.5%, Sumitomo 17.5%, A.H. Al-Sagar 5%. Government 60%.
  - **40-year ECWPA** with MEW.
  - The 50% IPO to citizens was oversubscribed (2019) and the company listed on Boursa Kuwait.
  - ACWA Power later bought ENGIE's 17.5%.

  Sources: [ENGIE newsroom (date n/c)](https://en.newsroom.engie.com/news/azzour-north-one-independent-water-power-project-in-kuwait-starts-full-commercial-operation-080d-314df.html); [Kuwait claims power IPO success, MEES, 2019-12-06](https://mees.com/2019/12/6/news-in-brief/kuwait-claims-power-ipo-success/f5ab1db0-1859-11ea-8142-b193c484c06c); [Acwa buys Engie stake, Gulf Construction Online (date n/c)](https://gulfconstructiononline.com/Article/388525/Acwa_buys_Engie_stake_in_Kuwait's_Az-Zour_project)
- **Tenor:** Solar IPPs use a 30-year PPA; IWPPs a 25-year ECWPA / BOT. AZN-1 had 40 years. Offtaker is MEWRE in each case (sources above).
- **Customs:** Zone 2 reportedly allows a customs exemption of up to 10% for 10 years (SolarQuarter, 2025-11-27).
- **[UNVERIFIED]:** PPA currency (KWD or USD indexation), the form of government guarantee or support, and the termination-payment regime. None of these appeared in accessible sources.

- **Record heat:** **53.9 °C at Mitribah, 21 July 2016**, verified by WMO (±0.1 °C). It is the highest recorded in Asia. [WMO verifies 3rd and 4th hottest temperature recorded on Earth, WMO, 2019-06-18](https://wmo.int/news/media-centre/wmo-verifies-3rd-and-4th-hottest-temperature-recorded-earth)

  - A KISR study compared ground data from five sites with SolarGIS, MERRA and NASA SSE. GHI agrees reasonably after site adaptation. **DNI estimates diverge significantly** because of aerosol uncertainty. [Solar Resource Assessment over Kuwait, EuroSun 2014 (ISES)](https://proceedings.ises.org/conference/eurosun2014/papers/eurosun2014-0137-AlRasheedi.pdf)
  - **[UNVERIFIED]:** GHI of about 1,900–2,100 kWh/m²/yr comes from Mordor Intelligence only. [Kuwait solar energy market, Mordor (date n/c)](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/kuwait-solar-energy-market). Global Solar Atlas figures for Shagaya were not retrieved; get them from globalsolaratlas.info.
- **Soiling:**
  - Shagaya PV requires monthly water cleaning ([RSER 2020](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/rensus/v133y2020ics1364032120304305.html)).
  - Kuwait studies report about 17% power loss after 6 days of exposure in one experiment, and 8.7–15.1% monthly-cleaning losses depending on season. [JEAS soiling table (date n/c)](https://jeas.springeropen.com/articles/10.1186/s44147-021-00052-6/tables/2)
  - **[UNVERIFIED]** A Shagaya-specific daily soiling rate.
- **Wind:** 7.9 m/s at 80 m at Shagaya (section 4).

## Implications for a MEWRE renewable PM

1. **MEWRE is the offtaker, not the procurer, for IPPs.** KAPP runs RFQ, RFP and evaluation. The PM's job is technical requirements, interface with the grid (MEWRE transmission), PPA performance and contract management over 25–30 years.
2. **The approval chain sets the critical path.** KAPP, then the Higher Committee, then the State Audit Bureau, with Fatwa and Legislation reviewing legal terms. Zone 1 slipped from June to about August 2026 at this stage. Build approval float into every schedule.
3. **Grid works are on the MEWRE side and are procured separately**, for example the Shagaya–Wafra 400 kV line under CAPT rules. A late grid connection can strand an IPP. Track interface dates under the PPA closely, because grid delay usually triggers deemed-energy or compensation exposure for the offtaker.
4. **Expect two procurement tracks:** competitive KAPP IPPs (Masdar, ACWA, EDF) and China G2G (SPIC/NEA). The PM must reconcile scope, land, grid capacity and dispatch between them.
5. **Know the procurement law change.** Law 94/2026 raises the direct-contract threshold to KD 1m, bans agents and tightens local content from about December 2026. This affects MEWRE's own EPC and services contracts.
6. **Prior review by the Audit Bureau applies to contracts of about KD 100k or more (verify).** Budget time for it on consultancy, O&M and grid contracts as well.
7. **Peak demand of about 17.5–17.7 GW against about 17 GW reliable capacity** makes renewables and storage a reliability issue, not only a climate issue. Solar output fits the 11:00–17:00 peak, but the evening peak needs BESS. The planned 1–1.5 GW BESS is strategically linked to the PV ramp.
8. **The design environment is extreme:** 50 °C+ ambient (53.9 °C record), heavy dust, monthly cleaning needs, and water scarcity. Specify high-temperature derating, robotic or dry cleaning, soiling monitoring and dust-resistant equipment. Require bankable P50/P90 using site-adapted irradiance data from KISR stations.
9. **Treat DNI-dependent (CSP) assumptions carefully.** KISR found satellite DNI diverges significantly in Kuwait. The CSP pilot's post-2019 performance is not public, which is a key lesson-learned gap.
10. **Tariff subsidy economics:** customers pay 2–5 fils/kWh while cost of supply is far higher. Every solar kWh displaces expensive liquid fuel and LNG. Frame business cases on avoided fuel (crude, gasoil, LNG) and summer import costs (GCCIA approvals of about KWD 169m in 2025).
11. **The PPP structure is distinctive:** a 50% citizen IPO plus a minority public entity share. Developers hold about 26–44% (40% in practice). Plan for project-company governance with KAPP as a major shareholder, and for eventual listing on Boursa Kuwait.
12. **Targets are ambitious against delivery.** The goal is 15% (or 30%) by 2030 and 50% by 2050, but installed renewables are only about 100 MW and Rystad projects about 7% by 2030. A PM should prioritise schedule certainty: land, grid, approvals.
13. **Local partners matter.** Each Zone 1 and Zone 2 bidder paired with a Kuwaiti group (Alghanim, Al-Sagar, Al-Kharafi and others), and the new law tightens local content. Monitor local-content and Kuwaitisation compliance in contract management.
14. **Keep stakeholders aligned:** MEWRE (offtaker and grid), KAPP, KISR (data and technical know-how), KPC/KOC (separate 1–2 GW captive solar plans that compete for land and grid), EPA (climate reporting), and the Supreme Council for Planning (Vision 2035).
15. **Re-verify before relying on anything** marked Med/Low or [UNVERIFIED] above, especially the Zone 1 award, tariffs, thresholds and China G2G details.
